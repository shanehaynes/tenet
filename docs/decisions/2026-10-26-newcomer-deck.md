# Slides rebuilt around a worked example for a newcomer (2026-10-26)

`docs/plan/evidence-report-slides.md` §3 fixed ten slides in the order of the report's graded sections. Slide 5 was the review table (S03), and slide 6 put the threshold chart beside the HW5 agent. Two read-only reviews on submission day, one from a technology journalist and one from a technical instructor, found that this deck reports results to a reader who already knows the method. The one-sentence definition was only in the speaker notes. The four types were named but not defined. S03 cannot be read at slide size. The captions pointed at "F1", "HW5" and run IDs without saying what they are. Both reviews are kept outside the repo; the changes they led to are below.

The deck keeps ten slides, but they now teach in order: what Tenet is; one real failure that pass/fail would miss (the clinic agent that refused, then escalated nothing, from run `JLX9iZjgpG`); the four types, each with an example rule and its test; the pipeline, with what each step produces; one rule traced end to end (the $10,000 sweep, S10); the real HW5 team (S20a in place of S20c); the ambiguity map with its legend in words; cost; trust (classifier and judge agreement, beside the safety screenshots); and what to remember, including the limits. S03 stays in the report only. Every number still comes from `report-data.json`. In the report, the same review replaced raw template strings with tables and plain sentences, and fixed the "1 collisions" plural at its source in `collect-evidence`.
