# Brightwater Collective
## Our Commitment to Responsible Technology

We believe technology should serve people, not the other way around. Our tools exist to make the work of our members a little lighter and their lives a little fuller, and we measure our success by the trust they place in us.

We believe in openness, curiosity and care. We hope for a future in which every member feels heard, every voice counts, and the technology we share reflects the values of the community that built it.
